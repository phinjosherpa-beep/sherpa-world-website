WELCOME TO SHERPA WORLD

A digital space to explore and share Sherpa heritage.

Sherpa World brings together digital resources related to the language, spiritual traditions and living culture of the Sherpa community. It is designed to make these resources easier to discover and explore on a mobile device.

Whether you are reconnecting with your roots or learning about Sherpa heritage for the first time, Sherpa World offers a place to begin.

WHY SHERPA WORLD

Keeping heritage connected to the future.

Language and cultural knowledge are carried forward through learning, practice and sharing. Sherpa World aims to support that connection by bringing resources together in a digital format.

Its focus is not only on access to information, but also on encouraging curiosity, appreciation and continuity across generations.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Connecting heritage
with the digital world.

Created by Affix Link with support from the Global Sherpa Association, Sherpa World is a mobile app dedicated to preserving Sherpa language, Buddhism, traditional songs, and culture. It brings scholarly digital dictionaries, sacred hymns, and prayer books together with community-contributed photography, literature, and films in one accessible archive.

A LIVING PROJECT

Preservation is an ongoing journey.

Sherpa World is intended to grow as resources are added and the application develops. Future plans include exploring AI-based applications and other technologies that may help people discover and engage with approved Sherpa World content.

These are future development directions, not a description of AI features currently available in the app.
