WELCOME TO SHERPA WORLD

A digital space to explore and share Sherpa heritage.

Sherpa World brings together digital resources related to the language, spiritual traditions and living culture of the Sherpa community. It is designed to make these resources easier to discover and explore on a mobile device.

Whether you are reconnecting with your roots or learning about Sherpa heritage for the first time, Sherpa World offers a place to begin.

EXPLORE THE APP

Five pathways into Sherpa heritage.

01

LANGUAGE

Sherpa Language

The app materials describe an interactive digital dictionary and phrasebook for vocabulary and everyday phrases across Sambota, Nepali and English scripts, with native-speaker audio recordings to support pronunciation.

  • Dictionary and phrasebook resources
  • Sambota, Nepali and English
  • Native-speaker pronunciation audio
02

BUDDHISM

Buddhism & Sacred Teachings

Explore resources about the history, philosophies and sacred pilgrimage heritage of Himalayan Buddhism, presented in English and Nepali in the supplied app description.

  • Teachings and Buddhist philosophy
  • History and sacred heritage
  • Pilgrimage-related resources
03

SACRED WORDS

Prayers & Mantras

The app materials describe traditional Buddhist prayer texts in Sambota, English and Nepali, accompanied by translations and audio chanting.

  • Prayer texts in three scripts/languages
  • Translations and explanations
  • Audio chanting resources
04

LIVING CULTURE

Culture, Customs & Festivals

Discover Sherpa ways of life through cultural photographs, articles, books and documentary videos, as described in the app materials.

  • Customs and festivals
  • Photographs and articles
  • Books and documentary videos
05

TRADITIONAL SONGS

Traditional Songs

The supplied app description highlights traditional Sherpa songs with audio and lyrics transcribed in Sambota, Nepali and English.

  • Traditional Sherpa songs
  • Audio and synchronized lyrics
  • Three-script lyric presentation

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Connecting heritage
with the digital world.

Affix Link Pvt. Ltd. is a technology company specializing in smart card solutions and software development. It provides tailored smart card systems and enterprise software designed to serve the needs of government and corporate sectors.

Global Sherpa Association (GSA) is a community organization committed to preserving, promoting, and strengthening Sherpa language, culture, traditions, and heritage among Sherpa communities around the world.

Smart Card

The Sherpa World Smart Card provides full access to the app and its available resources. Smart cards will be distributed worldwide through the Global Sherpa Association. Revenue generated through the distribution of smart cards will contribute to the continued development and improvement of the platform. A free version with limited content is also available to download.

A LIVING PROJECT

Preservation is an ongoing journey.

Sherpa World is intended to grow as resources are added and the application develops. Future plans include exploring AI-based applications and other technologies that may help people discover and engage with approved Sherpa World content.

These are future development directions, not a description of AI features currently available in the app.
