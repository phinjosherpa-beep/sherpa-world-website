WELCOME TO SHERPA WORLD

A digital space to discover, preserve and share Sherpa heritage.

Sherpa World brings together a growing collection of digital resources celebrating the Sherpa language, spiritual traditions and living culture. Through an accessible mobile platform, it makes these resources easier to discover, learn from and share with communities around the world.

Whether you are reconnecting with your Sherpa roots, deepening your understanding of your heritage, or discovering Sherpa culture for the first time, Sherpa World offers a welcoming place to explore, learn and connect.

EXPLORE THE APP

Five Pathways to Discover Sherpa Heritage

01

LANGUAGE

Sherpa Language

Discover and learn the Sherpa language through an interactive digital dictionary and phrasebook featuring vocabulary and everyday expressions in Sambhota, Nepali and English. Native-speaker audio recordings help learners develop accurate pronunciation and build confidence in using the language.

  • Digital dictionary and phrasebook – Explore vocabulary and everyday expressions.
  • Three language formats – Sambhota, Nepali and English.
  • Native-speaker audio – Listen, learn and improve pronunciation.
02

BUDDHISM

Buddhism & Sacred Teachings

Discover the wisdom and spiritual heritage of Himalayan Buddhism through resources exploring its teachings, philosophy, history and sacred pilgrimage sites. Presented in English and Nepali, these materials offer an accessible introduction to Buddhist knowledge and traditions.

  • Buddhist teachings and philosophy
  • History, traditions and sacred heritage
  • Sacred sites and pilgrimage resources
03

SACRED WORDS

Prayers & Mantras

Explore a collection of traditional Buddhist prayers and mantras in Sambhota, English and Nepali. With translations, and audio chanting, these resources offer an opportunity to learn, understand and engage with the spiritual traditions of Sherpa heritage.

  • Prayer texts: Available in Sambhota, English and Nepali
  • Translations: Understand the meaning and significance of prayers and mantras
  • Audio chanting: Listen, learn and follow traditional recitations.
04

LIVING CULTURE

Culture, Traditions & Festivals

Explore the richness of Sherpa life and cultural heritage through photographs, stories, articles, books and documentary films. Discover the customs, celebrations and traditions that continue to shape Sherpa identity across generations.

  • Traditional customs and cultural festivals
  • Photographs, stories and articles
  • Books, films and documentaries
05

TRADITIONAL SONGS

Traditional Songs

Experience the richness of Sherpa musical heritage through a collection of traditional songs, featuring audio recordings and lyrics presented in Sambhota, Nepali and English. Discover the songs that connect generations and keep Sherpa traditions alive.

  • Traditional Sherpa songs celebrating cultural heritage
  • Audio recordings with lyrics for an engaging listening experience
  • Multilingual lyrics presented in Sambhota, Nepali and English

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Bringing Sherpa Heritage
into the Digital World

Affix Link Pvt. Ltd. is a technology company specializing in smart card solutions and software development. It delivers innovative, customized digital solutions, including smart card systems and enterprise software, designed to meet the evolving needs of government institutions and corporate organizations.

Global Sherpa Association (GSA) is a community-based organization dedicated to preserving, promoting and strengthening Sherpa language, culture, traditions and heritage. Through community engagement and cultural initiatives, GSA works to keep Sherpa identity and heritage vibrant and connected across generations and around the world.

Smart Card

Full Access to Sherpa World

The Sherpa World Smart Card unlocks full access to the app and its digital resources. Through the Global Sherpa Association's worldwide network, the cards will be made available to Sherpa communities and interested users around the world.

Revenue generated from Smart Card distribution will support the ongoing development, maintenance and enhancement of the platform, helping ensure that Sherpa heritage remains accessible to future generations.

A free version of the app, offering limited content, is also available for anyone who wishes to explore Sherpa World.

A collective effort, a shared heritage.

Sherpa World is the result of the collective contributions of many individuals and organizations. We are deeply grateful to all who have generously shared their knowledge, creativity, resources and support in making this initiative possible.
